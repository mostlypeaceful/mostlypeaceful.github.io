## About Me

I have over 25 years of experience in game development, contributing to 20+ AAA titles and flagship franchises like **Need for Speed**, **Medal of Honor**, **SOCOM** at **EA, THQ, Zipper Interactive, and Snowblind Studios**. Founded **Signal Studios**, leading to the creation and release of 8 original games, including the award-nominated and multi-million selling **Toy Soldiers**. **Signal Studios** was recognized as one of the top 30 game development studios worldwide in 2011.

Signal closed its doors due to a combination of my own mistakes and a few external factors. I am a bit older and A.I. has pivoted from being useless to most useful. So what now? Exploring employment opportunities in traditional game development, web-based projects, A.I. integration, A.I. development and still hoping for a Toy Soldiers sequel while working on side projects.
