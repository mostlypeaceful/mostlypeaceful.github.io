SUMMARY#

Welcome. My name is D.R. and I am a game studio founder, creative director and hands-on game developer with over 25 years of experience developing games at EA, Snowblind Studios, Zipper Interactive, and THQ. After founding Signal Studios and leading the Toy Soldiers franchise to 2 D.I.C.E. nominations and millions in sales, I am currently focused on indie game development, creating a historical RPG concerning the American Indian Wars. I specialize in bridging the gap between old-school game development techniques and modern technologies to build authentic games without large teams or technological bloat.
